# Cryptography Concepts

## Overview

This room introduces the fundamental concepts of **cryptography** and explains how cryptographic techniques are used to protect information and communications.

## Key Takeaways

* Cryptography is used to protect information from unauthorized access or modification.
* **Plaintext** is the original readable information before encryption.
* **Ciphertext** is the encrypted form of information.
* **Encryption** converts plaintext into ciphertext, while **decryption** converts ciphertext back into plaintext.
* Cryptographic algorithms use **keys** to control encryption and decryption.
* **Symmetric encryption** uses the same key for encryption and decryption.
* **AES (Advanced Encryption Standard)** is a widely used symmetric encryption algorithm.
* **Asymmetric encryption** uses two keys: a **public key** and a **private key**.
* Public and private keys can be used to securely exchange information and verify identities.
* **Hashing** converts data into a fixed-length value and is designed to be a one-way process.
* Hashes can help verify the integrity of files and information.
* Cryptography plays an important role in protecting the **confidentiality and integrity** of data.
