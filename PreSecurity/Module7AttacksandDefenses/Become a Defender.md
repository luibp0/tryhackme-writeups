# Become a Defender

## Overview

This room introduces the role of a **cybersecurity defender** and explains how defensive security teams work to protect systems, networks, and data from cyber threats.

It focuses on the defender mindset, monitoring, detection, incident response, and the importance of understanding attacker behavior in order to build stronger defenses.

## Objectives

* Understand the role of a cybersecurity defender.
* Learn the difference between offensive and defensive security.
* Understand the defender mindset.
* Learn the basics of monitoring and threat detection.
* Understand the importance of incident response.
* Explore common defensive security responsibilities.
* Learn how defenders use information about attacks to improve security.

## Key Takeaways

* Cybersecurity defenders focus on protecting systems, networks, and information from threats.
* Defensive security involves monitoring environments for suspicious or malicious activity.
* Security alerts can help defenders identify possible attacks or policy violations.
* Defenders investigate suspicious activity to determine whether an incident is a **true positive** or **false positive**.
* Incident response involves identifying, containing, investigating, and recovering from security incidents.
* Understanding attacker techniques helps defenders recognize and stop malicious behavior.
* Defensive security teams may use technologies such as **SIEM, firewalls, endpoint security tools, and threat intelligence**.
* Effective defense depends on **people, processes, and technology** working together.
* Defensive security plays an important role in protecting the **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA)** of systems and information.
