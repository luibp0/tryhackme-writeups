# The CIA Triad

## Overview

This room introduces the **CIA Triad**, one of the fundamental models used in cybersecurity to understand and protect information and systems.

The CIA Triad consists of three core security principles: **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability**. These principles help organizations identify security requirements and understand the impact of different threats and attacks.

## Objectives

* Understand the purpose of the CIA Triad.
* Learn the meaning of Confidentiality.
* Understand the importance of Integrity.
* Learn how Availability affects systems and services.
* Identify threats that can affect each CIA Triad principle.
* Understand how security controls help protect information.

## Key Takeaways

* The **CIA Triad** consists of Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
* **Confidentiality** ensures that information is accessible only to authorized users.
* Encryption, authentication, and access controls can help protect confidentiality.
* **Integrity** ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.
* Hashing and access controls can help verify and protect data integrity.
* **Availability** ensures that systems, services, and information remain accessible when authorized users need them.
* Attacks such as **Denial of Service (DoS)** and **Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)** can negatively affect availability.
* Security incidents can affect one or multiple parts of the CIA Triad.
* Understanding the CIA Triad helps security professionals evaluate threats, risks, and appropriate security controls.
