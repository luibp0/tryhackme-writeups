# 🕵️ Management Wants a Word

## Description

**Management Wants a Word** concludes the Hacker Holidays 2026 event with a digital forensic investigation focused on uncovering the truth behind suspicious activity within an organization. By examining forensic artifacts and reconstructing system events, the investigation reveals how seemingly legitimate actions can conceal deliberate malicious behavior.

---

## Objectives

- Perform a structured digital forensic investigation.
- Analyze system artifacts to reconstruct user activity.
- Identify evidence of suspicious or unauthorized actions.
- Correlate forensic findings to establish a timeline.
- Understand the importance of evidence-based incident response.

## Key Takeaways

- Digital forensics relies on correlating evidence from multiple system artifacts rather than isolated findings.
- Building an accurate timeline is essential for understanding the sequence of events during an investigation.
- Registry hives, event logs, and user artifacts provide valuable insight into system activity.
- Effective incident response depends on thorough evidence collection, analysis, and documentation.
