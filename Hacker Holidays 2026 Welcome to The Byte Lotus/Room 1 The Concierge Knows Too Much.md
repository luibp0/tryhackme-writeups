# 🛎️ The Concierge Knows Too Much

## Description

The first room of **Hacker Holidays 2026** introduces the **Byte Lotus Hotel** and begins the investigation into its suspicious digital infrastructure. The challenge focuses on gathering publicly available information and interacting with the hotel's AI-powered concierge to uncover information that should not be publicly accessible.

Through careful observation and questioning, the concierge reveals hidden details and confidential instructions, demonstrating the risks of excessive information disclosure.

---

## Objectives

* Investigate the Byte Lotus Hotel.
* Gather publicly available information.
* Interact with the AI concierge.
* Identify information disclosure vulnerabilities.
* Retrieve the hidden instructions.

## Key Takeaways

* Publicly available information can reveal more than expected.
* AI assistants may unintentionally expose sensitive information when insufficient safeguards are in place.
* Careful questioning and observation are valuable skills during reconnaissance.
* Small pieces of leaked information can become useful intelligence during later stages of an assessment.
