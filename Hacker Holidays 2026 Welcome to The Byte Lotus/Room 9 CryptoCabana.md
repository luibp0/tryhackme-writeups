# 🔐 CryptoCabana

## Description

**CryptoCabana** introduces the importance of cryptographic security by exploring how improperly protected secrets and weak key management can compromise sensitive operations. The room demonstrates the risks of exposing confidential data and highlights the role of cryptography in maintaining integrity, authenticity, and confidentiality.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze the application's use of cryptographic mechanisms.
- Identify insecure secret storage practices.
- Understand the impact of exposed cryptographic material.
- Investigate authentication and integrity validation.
- Assess security risks related to poor key management.

---

- Key Management

- Security Assessment

---

## Key Takeaways

- Cryptographic systems are only as secure as the way secrets and keys are protected.
- Exposed credentials or private keys can completely undermine application security.
- Proper key management is essential for maintaining confidentiality and authenticity.
- Secure storage and handling of cryptographic material are critical security practices.
